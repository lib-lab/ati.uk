# Freight board prototype

**English** · [Русский](README.ru.md)

> Prototype: https://ati-uk.pages.dev · tester guide (in Russian): https://ati-uk.pages.dev/guide (the only document available publicly)
>
> Overview for people who check user journeys and UX, no development background needed: [docs/OVERVIEW.md](docs/OVERVIEW.md).

A clickable prototype of a road freight exchange in the form of a listings board. It is a sandbox for checking the entities, rules and scenarios of [docs/TZ.md](docs/TZ.md). There is no backend: data lives in memory and is saved to `localStorage`. The interface is in English and Russian; the project documents in `docs/` are in Russian.

## Running

Requires Node.js 20+.

```bash
npm install
npm run dev        # http://localhost:5173
npm test           # unit tests (Vitest)
npm run build      # type check and production build
npm run build:preview  # one self-contained file dist/preview.html to show without a server
```

Without a local setup, the prototype opens at https://ati-uk.pages.dev.

## Scenario tools

All tools are in the grey "Test tools" bar under the top bar. On a narrow screen (< 640 px) the bar folds into one line with the current company and date; tapping it unfolds the bar.

- **Log in as.** A drop-down or the "Log in as" page: any company from the seed or the moderator in one click. The "Seed profile" column describes which scenario a company stands for. A new company can be registered with the "Register a company" link on the "Log in as" page. Companies c22–c33 are background clients of Moravia Trans and Euro Cargo Partners: they have no active listings and exist for the "company with history" scenario. Switching counts as the company's login.
- **Time control.** The current (simulated) date and the buttons "+1 hour", "+1 day", "+1 week", "+6 months". Start: 5 Oct 2026, 09:00 UTC. A shift recalculates ratings (tenure, review age and company age); the changes go to the log with the reason "Daily recalculation". Listings whose lifetime is over get the status "expired" and disappear from search; the owner gets a notification.
- **Test balance.** The "Balance" page: buttons "+ €10 / €50 / €100" (through the stub payment adapter), the free contacts of the month, the contact package (purchase, remaining contacts, validity), the transaction history. The balance and free contacts of the current company are also shown on the right of the top bar.
- **Event log.** The "Event log" page: every contact opening, payment, top-up, login, time shift and rating change with its time and reason. Rating changes show the score before and after and the components that changed. Filters by event type and by company.
- **Subscriptions.** "Save as subscription" on the search page (with the choice "Instantly" / "Hourly digest" next to it); the list is under "Subscriptions" in the top bar. E-mails are addressed to the test inbox 90569oc62@mozmail.com but go to a stub mail adapter (they are not actually sent); the "Event log" shows "E-mail sent" events with the address.
- **Notifications.** The "Notifications" page with an unread counter and links to listings: listing reminders and expiry notices, subscriptions, complaints, claims, refunds, edits of opened listings, slowed-down contact openings.
- **Simulate a bot.** The current company tries to open up to 6 contacts of different companies in a row, bypassing the listing cards and the "I am a human" check, like a scraper. The result is shown next to the button. Money and free contacts are charged as usual.
- **Reset data.** The "Reset data" button (with a confirmation) brings back the initial seed. The seed is deterministic: after a reset the state is always the same.

## Mandatory scenarios

The ten scenarios of the ROADMAP. All of them pass by the steps below on a clean seed ("Reset data"); checked in a browser at 1280 and 390 px wide.

1. A carrier finds cargo with filters, sees the rating, opens the contact, the balance is charged, a repeat opening is free — "A carrier finds cargo and opens the contact", "Search with filters".
2. Free contacts are used up: a clear state and an offer to top up the balance — "Contacts used up, no money".
3. A shipper makes a listing open and turns on priority placement — "Open listing and priority" (for example, as Silesia Steel).
4. A review only after opening a contact, no more often than the brief's rule — "Review: both sides of a contact, 'did the transport take place?'".
5. A "contact does not answer" complaint confirmed by the moderator: the payment is refunded and the complaint counts — "Refund for a contact": Nordic Reefer and Fresh Start Transport open the contact of Rhein Spedition and report "Contact does not answer", the moderator confirms both, and both companies get a refund.
6. Red flag: a warning at the contact button — "Red flag".
7. A new member without reviews: a neutral mark, the score from the available components — "New member".
8. Search subscription: a new listing appears in notifications — "'Instantly' subscription".
9. A listing expires after a time shift, the owner extends it — "Expiry and extension".
10. A simple moderator page: complaints, claims, flags — "Moderation" as the moderator.

## Implemented scenarios

Numbers in the steps are for a clean seed (the "Reset data" button).

- Switching between companies ("log in as"), keeping the state across reloads, resetting the data.
- **A carrier finds cargo and opens the contact.** Log in as Danube Haulage (carrier, 3 free contacts, €25) → Search → Cargo tab → filter From = Warsaw → open a listing. The score, the "Verified" mark or a flag and the number of reviews are shown next to the button. Clicking the score unfolds the components. The first 3 openings are free, the fourth charges €5 from the balance. A repeat contact with this company is free on any of its listings (for companies Danube already worked with in the seed data, the contact is open at once).
- **Search with filters.** Search → filters: from and to with a radius in km (From = Warsaw, ± 150 adds cargo from Łódź), a date or a date range, weight and volume "from — to", body type, loading method, minimum rating, "Only with reviews (3+)", "Only without red flags" (removes Ghost Lines). The minimum rating does not cut off newcomers without a rating: at a minimum of 90, Euro Cargo Partners and QuickRate Logistics, for example, stay in the results with the "New member" mark; "Only with reviews" removes them. Sorting: by loading date, rate per km, rating, freshness; priority listings always come first. When nothing is found, an empty state with a hint.
- **Posting a listing.** Log in as Silesia Steel → "Post a listing". Required fields are marked "*"; an empty submit highlights the fields. A shipper posts only cargo, a carrier only trucks, a forwarder both. The route can be saved as a template and used to start a new form.
- **Open listing and priority.** "My listings" → "Make open · €10" and "Priority placement · €15": the balance goes down by €25, the listing is first in the results with the "Priority" mark. Log in as Nordic Reefer (it has no opened contacts) → the card: "Show contact", the contact is free and no free contacts are used.
- **Expiry and extension.** Post cargo loading tomorrow → "+1 day" twice → a reminder "expires on…" in "Notifications" → one more "+1 day" → an expiry notification, the listing is gone from search, in "My listings" on the "Removed and expired" tab → "Extend" (a new loading date can be set) → back in search; priority is not carried over, "Open" is kept.
- **Removal with a reason.** "My listings" → "Remove" → one of three reasons. The reason is shown in the list and in the "Event log" and stays a metric: it does not affect the rating (brief).
- **Contacts used up, no money.** Log in as Moravia Trans (3 of 3 free contacts used, €0) → open cargo of a company Moravia has not worked with yet: the button is disabled with an explanation → "Top up balance" → "+ €10" → back to the card → the contact opens for €5, the balance is €5. The "Event log" shows the top-up, the payment and the contact opening.
- **Time shift and rating recalculation.** "+6 months" → "Event log" → type "Rating changed": companies' scores changed with the reason "Daily recalculation (182 days)", the changed components are shown. Free contacts are renewed (new month), listings expired: search shows the empty state.
- **Red flag.** A listing of Ghost Lines UAB (liquidated): a warning at the button, opening needs a second confirmation.
- **Company profile with the score explained.** A company name in the results, on the card ("Company profile") or on the "Log in as" page leads to the profile: the score, marks and flags with explanations, four components with their nominal and effective weight and contribution to the score, a breakdown of each component and the history of its changes, reviews (the weight of each and the reasons it is lowered, "No deal: contact rated" for reviews without a deal), claims with statuses and weights, registry data (age and reports: "shown for information, not scored"). Examples: Danube Haulage — two recent unconfirmed claims of −25 each, "Claims" = 50; Fast Profit Freight — a claim older than 30 days "no longer counts"; Lowland Cargo — the registry does not report the status, "Official check" = 100 ("does not lower the score"); Vistula Logistics — "Activity": tenure and 11 contacts that led to reviews.
- **Review: both sides of a contact, "did the transport take place?".** Log in as Nordic Reefer → Rhein Spedition's profile: instead of the form, "Only the sides of a contact can rate each other…". Open the contact on any Rhein cargo → "Leave a review or a claim" → "Did the transport take place? Yes" → 2★ → Rhein's score 95 → 94 (the author is a newcomer, the review weighs little). A repeat review is unavailable until 3 Jan 2027; after "+6 months" it is available again. Log in as Rhein → Nordic Reefer's profile: Rhein, whose contact was opened, can rate Nordic too; with the answer "No" the hint says "rate only the contact… not agreeing on the price is not a reason for a low mark", and the review is marked "No deal: contact rated". Rhein → "Reply" under the review about itself (once).
- **Claim.** In the same place (Nordic Reefer → Rhein), "File a claim" → Rhein 94 → 92: the claim weighs like a newcomer's review (weight 0.4, −9.9 points in the "Claims" component). Rhein gets a notification and can answer ("Answer the claim"). An unconfirmed claim stops counting after 30 days; the moderator can confirm it ("Moderation" → "Claims" → "Confirm claim"), and then it counts until settled. "Mark as settled" (by the claimant or the moderator) → the score comes back.
- **Complaint and the company's answer.** Log in as Nordic Reefer, open the contact of a Rhein cargo → "Report this listing" on the card: "Listing is outdated" (for everyone), "Contact does not answer" and "Contact data is wrong" (after opening the contact). Rhein gets a notification, sees the complaint in "My listings" and answers it ("Answer") before the decision. The moderator ("Moderation" → "Complaints") sees the company's answer, the complaint share and what will be refunded on confirmation.
- **Refund for a contact.** "Contact data is wrong" (the number does not exist): after the moderator confirms, the money or the free contact is returned at once. "Contact does not answer" from one company gives no refund ("no refund yet: 1.0 of 2 independent confirmed reports needed"); when the same complaint about the same company is confirmed for a second independent company, both get a refund. Companies sharing a device count as one. At most 3 refunds per account in 30 days; complaints of a reporter with a high share of refunds weigh 0.5.
- **Hiding listings by complaint share.** Listings are hidden only when confirmed complaints make up ≥ 20 % of the company's opened contacts in 30 days and there are at least 3 of them (for example, three "Listing is outdated" complaints about Polska Agro from three companies, confirmed by the moderator). The owner gets a notification → "My listings" → "Contact data is up to date" → the listings are back in search.
- **Disputes.** The "Dispute" button is at rating events in a component's "Changes" (for your own company), at your own flags in the profile, at complaints about your listings in "My listings" and at claims against you in the profile. Until the decision, "Disputed: waiting for the moderator" is shown next to it. Moderator → "Moderation" → "Disputes": "Accept" removes the flag or rejects the complaint or claim, "Reject" leaves it as is; the company gets a notification.
- **Registry mismatch.** Euro Cargo Partners' profile: yellow "Clarify your data" until 14 Oct 2026. Log in as Euro Cargo → the "Use the registry name…" button → the flag is closed, "Verified". Without the fix, "+1 week" twice → the flag turns red, "Data not confirmed", and the company gets a notification.
- **Inactive company.** Riga Container (last login ~5.5 months ago). As any other company (logging in as Riga would update its login date): "+1 week" → Riga gets the warning "will be hidden from search on 23 Oct 2026"; "+1 week" twice more → Riga is hidden from search, the rating is kept. Log in as Riga → "Notifications" has the warning and the hiding notice, the banner on top says "Your company is hidden…" → "Confirm contacts and show my company" → back in search, without a new check.
- **New member.** A listing of Nordic Reefer OÜ (Trucks tab): the neutral "New member" mark; the score is based on the check and claims, reviews and activity are marked "no data yet".
- **Shared device: no flag.** QuickRate Trans and QuickRate Logistics work from one device and rated each other: no flag, their mutual reviews weigh less ("shared device, mutual ×0.3").
- **Closed ring and review weight instead of punishment.** Northline Freight's profile (ring c19–c21: three new companies that rated only each other 5★): each review weighs ≈ 0.12 ("author with little history", "closed group"), the "Reviews" component is 60, not 100. Moderator → "Flags" → "Run now" (or "+1 day"): the ring gets "Under review", reviews inside the ring get weight 0, "Reviews" drops to the neutral 50 (a flag cannot raise the score); reviews by ring members about companies outside the ring count. "Confirm manipulation" → red "Blocked", reviews inside the ring are voided. Honest partners with mutual reviews keep full weight: Vistula Logistics and Baltic Freight rated each other 5★, Vistula's review weighs 1.00, Baltic's 0.50 only because of its age; across the seed there are 26 mutual pairs, and none is lowered as a closed group.
- **Hidden listings.** Listings of the blocked Fast Profit Freight are not shown in the results.
- **Listing edit, history and notifications.** Log in as Nordic Reefer → Search → Cargo → Rhein Spedition's cargo (Frankfurt → Warsaw) → "Open contact" (free). Log in as Rhein → the card → "Edit" (or "My listings" → "Edit") → change the weight and the comment → "Save changes". The card's "Change history" has an entry marked "Key field": "Weight, t: 13.6 t → 15.6 t", "Comment: …". Log in as Nordic Reefer → "Notifications": "Listing … that you opened was changed: weight, t; comment". If only the comment changed, there is no "Key field" mark and no right to a refund.
- **Refund after a key edit.** Continuing the previous one: Nordic Reefer → this cargo's card → "The route, dates, body type, weight or rate changed within 24 hours…" → "Get the payment back": free contacts are 3/3 again (with a payment from the balance, €5 would come back). After "+1 day" a new edit no longer gives a right to a refund.
- **The contact is hidden until opened.** Neither the name nor the phone is shown in the results or on the card before opening: the card data comes without the contact person, and only the domain access check gives out the contact. If access to the company is already open through another of its listings, the new card has a "Show contact" button: free, but the reveal is recorded so the company's edits of this listing are notified as well.
- **Contact collection: slow-down, then a block.** Log in as Vistula Logistics → "Simulate a bot": "Bot: 5 opened" — after 5 openings without the card the company is slowed down and gets a notification. The card of any company Vistula has not worked with yet: "I am a human, not a program" → the contact opens; without the tick the button is disabled, at most 5 openings per hour; "+1 hour" allows 5 more. "Simulate a bot" again: attempts without the check go on → "Opening contacts is blocked". Moderator → "Moderation" → "Contact collection": the reasons and "Unblock".
- **Daily limit.** "Account" → "N of 50 new contacts today". Moravia Trans or Euro Cargo Partners (more than 3 months on the platform and 21 opened contacts against the threshold of 20) → "Raise to 150" → the limit is 150 without review. For new companies the button is disabled with an explanation of what is missing.
- **Consent to show personal data.** Log in as Rhein → "Account" → "Withdraw consent" for Jonas Becker. Log in as Nordic Reefer (contact opened) → a Rhein cargo card: "The contact person withdrew consent…", no name or phone. Rhein → "Give consent" → the contact is visible again. The consent text is a placeholder ("Consent text").
- **Data export.** "Account" → "Request my data" → type the company name (case does not matter; until the name matches, the button is disabled) → "Prepare the data": JSON with all the company's data in a field with a "Copy" button (and a file download if the host allows it). The "Event log" shows "Data downloaded".
- **Account deletion.** "Account" → "Delete account" → type the company name (for example, "rhein spedition gmbh"; "Rhein" does not match) → "Delete": contact persons are removed, active listings are taken down, logging in as the company is no longer possible (the current user switches to the moderator). Profile: "Account deleted on …"; reviews, claims and the rating history are kept and linked to the company without a person's name.
- **Retention of the opening log.** Records of the contact opening log older than 12 months are anonymised on a time shift (some records in the seed are already anonymised). "+6 months" twice → the "Event log" shows "N contact openings older than 12 months anonymised".
- **People's names in reviews.** The review and claim forms warn not to write people's names and phone numbers. There is no automatic cleanup. Delete Rhein Spedition (see "Account deletion") → moderator → Vistula Logistics' profile → at Rhein's review, "Hide text (personal data request)" → "Requested by": Rhein Spedition GmbH → "Hide text". Instead of the text: "Text hidden on … at the request of a former user"; the stars and Vistula's score (95) are unchanged; the "Event log" shows "Review text hidden". While there are no deleted accounts, the action is unavailable.
- **"Instantly" subscription.** Log in as Baltic Freight (carrier) → Search → Cargo → From = Warsaw, Body type = Tent → "Instantly" → "Save as subscription". Saving the same filters again: "A subscription with these filters already exists". Log in as Polska Agro → post cargo Warsaw → Berlin, Tent. Log in as Baltic Freight → "Notifications": "New listing for your subscription: Warsaw → Berlin (…)" with an "Open …" link. Cargo Kraków → Berlin or Warsaw → Berlin with a Reefer body gives no notification; neither does your own listing.
- **Digest subscription.** Log in as Moravia Trans → the same filters → "Hourly digest" → save. Polska Agro posts two matching cargo listings. Moravia has no notifications; "Subscriptions" shows "2 waiting for the next digest". "+1 hour" → one notification "2 new listings for your subscriptions: …" with two links.
- **Edit and subscription.** Cargo from Prague does not match a "From = Warsaw" subscription; the owner changes the loading city to Warsaw ("Edit") → the subscriber gets a notification. Further edits of this listing give no more notifications.
- **E-mails and managing subscriptions.** "Event log" → type "E-mail sent": an e-mail for each subscription notification. "Subscriptions": the filters as text, how many listings have come, a frequency change, "Open in search" (search opens with the saved filters), "Delete" with a confirmation.
- **Registration and the registry check.** "Log in as" → "Register a company". The test registry decides by the last digit of the number: a regular number (for example, PL NIP 7011234562) → "Verified", and the new company becomes the current one at once; …9 → red "Not recommended"; …8 → "Clarify your data: the registry name is …" with a date (after 14 days the flag turns red; fix it with the button in the profile); …0 → "not found", the same yellow flag. Without the consent tick: "Consent is required…"; the new contact person's consent date is shown in "Account". The number of Vistula Logistics (PL, 525-123-45-61 in any spelling) → "This number is already registered in PL: one company — one account".
- **Role change.** A registered carrier company → "Post a listing": trucks only. Your profile → "Role" → Shipper → "Change role" → "Post a listing": cargo only. The "Event log" shows "Role of … changed".
- **Daily registry check.** On every "+1 day" the registry is checked against all companies that are not deleted: the seed is stable (no new registry flags); if the registry starts reporting a liquidation or another name, the flag appears, and when the data match again it closes (covered by tests; in the interface the registry stub does not change).
- **Contact package.** Log in as Moravia Trans (€0, free contacts of the month used up) → "Balance" → "Buy a package · €70" → "Not enough balance" → "+ €100" → "Buy a package": the balance is €30, the top bar shows "Package 20/20 until 4 Nov 2026"; a second package cannot be bought while this one is valid. The cargo card of a company Moravia has not worked with yet: "Open contact · from package", "19 of 20 left after this" → open: the package is 19/20, the balance is still €30. "+1 week" × 5 → the package has expired and is gone from the top bar; in the new month there are 3 free contacts first, then an opening charges the balance.
- **Refund to the package.** A contact opened from the package goes back to it (remaining +1) on a confirmed "Contact data is wrong" complaint or a key edit of the listing within 24 hours, while the package is valid. The moderator sees "the contact goes back to the package"; for an expired package, "no refund: the package has expired".
- **Russian interface.** The "EN — RU" toggle in the top bar → all labels, hints, notifications, reasons in the "Event log" and e-mails are in Russian; dates like "5 окт. 2026", decimals with a comma ("13,6 т"), "Тестовые инструменты" instead of "Test tools". Reload the page: the language is kept; "Сбросить данные" (Reset data) does not change the language. The same toggle switches back. Demo data (companies, cities, review texts) stays as is.

## Known limitations

- **No server.** The whole state lives in one user's browser (localStorage); "log in as" needs no password. The access rules (contact only after opening, limits, automatic collection) are checked in the domain layer, but the contact data technically sits in the browser state: in a real system the server does the same check.
- **Simulated time** stands still between clicks. The smallest step is "+1 hour"; "within minutes" signals (a burst of openings) are counted by equal timestamps.
- **External systems are stubs.** The registry decides by the last digit of the number; only the stub's registry check is synchronous. Payments are in test mode. E-mails are addressed to the test inbox 90569oc62@mozmail.com but are not sent; there is no second channel (push, messenger). The "I am a human" check is a checkbox instead of a captcha.
- **Legal texts** (consent, policy) are placeholders. The data export cannot be downloaded as a file in the preview: the viewer blocks downloads, so the data is taken from the text field.
- **Scale.** Ratings and the ring detector are recalculated on the fly from all the data: instant for the seed, but thousands of companies would need a cache. The event log grows without limit, and localStorage holds a few megabytes: after long use, "Reset data" may be needed.
- **The mobile version is basic:** results as cards, folded tools and filters, no horizontal scrolling; the navigation wraps into several lines.
- **One currency (EUR)**; distances are straight-line × 1.2. Moderator decisions are not signed by a specific moderator; response deadlines are not modelled.
- **Checked** in Chromium (Playwright) at 1280 and 390 px wide; other browsers were not checked.
